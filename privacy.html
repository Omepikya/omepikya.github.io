Privacy Policy

Omepikya Expense is designed to help you manage your personal finances while treating financial information with care. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may handle, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Information You Provide

When you use Omepikya Expense, you may provide information needed to use the application and its features.

  • Account information used for authentication and account management.
  • Financial records such as income, expenses, amounts, dates, categories, notes, and related transaction information you choose to enter.
  • Information associated with financial tools you choose to use, such as budgets, savings goals, recurring transactions, reminders, subscriptions, or shared expenses.
  • Receipt or transaction information when you choose to use supported receipt or transaction-entry features.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting Omepikya for support.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide, maintain, and improve the functionality of Omepikya Expense.

Depending on the features you use, information may be used for:

  • Creating and displaying your financial records.
  • Calculating summaries, analytics, budgets, trends, and other financial information inside the application.
  • Providing reminders, alerts, and other requested financial tools.
  • Authenticating your account and providing account-based functionality.
  • Synchronizing supported information through Omepikya's cloud services when cloud functionality is enabled or used.
  • Diagnosing problems, responding to support requests, and improving reliability.

3. Local Device Information

Some information may be stored or processed locally on your Android device to support application functionality.

Local storage may allow the application to operate efficiently and maintain information required by features you use.

Information stored locally is subject to the security and access controls provided by your device and the Android environment.

You should keep your device protected with an appropriate screen lock and avoid giving unauthorized people access to it.

4. Cloud Synchronization

Omepikya Expense includes cloud-enabled functionality. When you use features that require synchronization, relevant application information may be transmitted to and stored by backend services used to provide those features.

Cloud access is associated with authenticated accounts. The purpose of synchronization is to provide supported connected financial experiences rather than to make your financial information publicly available.

5. Authentication and Account Security

Account-based features require authentication. Authentication information is used to establish and protect access to your account and supported services.

Where biometric protection is available in the application and you choose to enable it, the biometric mechanism is provided through supported Android biometric authentication facilities.

Omepikya does not need to receive your fingerprint or face biometric data to provide this local authentication mechanism.

6. Network and Technical Information

Using cloud and network-enabled features necessarily involves technical communication between the application and its backend services.

Technical information may be processed as necessary to authenticate requests, synchronize data, maintain service reliability, and investigate technical problems.

Omepikya does not ask users to provide unnecessary sensitive information simply to use basic expense-tracking functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

Some application functionality may rely on third-party technology or infrastructure providers.

These may include services supporting authentication, cloud infrastructure, Android platform functionality, barcode or text-recognition functionality, or network communication.

Such providers may process information according to the role they perform and their applicable terms and privacy policies.

Omepikya does not represent third-party services as being owned or controlled by Omepikya.

8. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Omepikya does not make your financial records public as part of normal use of the application.

Information may be processed by service providers where this is necessary to provide features you use, maintain the service, or protect the application and its users.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, and integrity of Omepikya, its users, or the service.

9. Your Choices and Responsibilities

  • You choose what financial information to enter into the application.
  • You are responsible for keeping your account credentials and device access secure.
  • You should not enter information that you do not need for the feature you are using.
  • You should review your account and application settings and use available controls to manage your information.
  • If you need help with account or data-related requests, contact Omepikya using the support address below.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the services and features you use, maintain account functionality, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

If you want to request deletion of account-related or personal information, contact us.

We may need to verify the request and may retain information where retention is required or permitted by applicable law.

11. Children's Privacy

Omepikya Expense is intended for people who are legally able to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the service.

12. Security

We use security-conscious application, authentication, network, and data-handling practices.

However, no internet-connected or electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should also protect their devices, credentials, and account access.

For additional information, see the Security section on the Omepikya website.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Omepikya Expense and its services evolve.

When material changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.
